Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective: 17 October 2026

NerdVerse India is the trade name of Zineeya Roy Chowdhury, a proprietorship. GSTIN: 29BGYPR0687R1ZT. Orders are shipped from Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

Shipping method

Orders are shipped through registered domestic courier companies and/or India Post Speed Post only. We select the carrier according to destination serviceability and parcel requirements.

Delivery area

We deliver across India, subject to courier serviceability for the destination PIN code. International shipping is not currently offered. If a paid order cannot be serviced, we will contact you and refund the amount collected for the unfulfilled order.

Dispatch and delivery estimates

For in-stock products, orders are dispatched within 10 calendar days of order confirmation and receipt of payment, whichever is later, unless a different dispatch or delivery date was agreed at order confirmation. The dispatch window is separate from the courier’s transit time. Estimated transit time after dispatch is 3–10 business days, depending on the destination. Remote locations may take longer. Business days exclude Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays. Pre-order or made-to-order products, if offered, will state a separate lead time on the listing before purchase.

Transit estimates depend on courier or postal operating norms and are not guaranteed unless expressly agreed. Courier or postal delays outside our reasonable control may affect delivery. We will help trace delayed shipments and communicate material delays and available resolutions. This does not exclude our obligations for undelivered orders or remedies available under applicable law. Email support if tracking has stopped updating or the estimated delivery window has passed.

Shipping charges

Standard shipping is free for orders with a merchandise total above ₹2,999 after discounts. For orders of ₹2,999 or less, shipping is calculated according to the delivery PIN code and parcel details and disclosed before payment. Any additional shipping charge requires your agreement before payment.

Refund of shipping charges

Original shipping charges are not refundable for an approved voluntary size/fit return after the order has been shipped. For a full order cancelled before dispatch, or cancelled by us without fulfilment, any shipping amount collected for that order is refunded. For a verified wrong-item, defect or misdescription claim, shipping costs are handled under our Returns, Replacements & Refund Policy and applicable law. No blanket shipping-charge exclusion limits a refund required by law.

Tracking

Once the courier collects the parcel, we will provide the courier name and AWB/tracking number through the available order communication channel. Tracking may take 24–48 hours to update after collection. Order and dispatch confirmations are sent to the email address provided with the order; delivery status is communicated when available from the carrier. Ensure that your email address is correct and check your spam folder.

Address and unsuccessful delivery

All orders are delivered to the address supplied by the buyer at purchase. Provide a complete address, correct PIN code and reachable phone number. Request address corrections immediately by email before dispatch. Corrections after dispatch depend on the courier. If a parcel is returned after an incorrect address, refusal or unsuccessful delivery attempts, contact us to arrange the next step; any proposed re-shipping charges will be disclosed before you agree.

Damaged, missing or undelivered parcels

Retain packaging and report the issue promptly with the order number and tracking reference. For delivered parcels, record a continuous unedited unboxing video showing the unopened parcel, label, seals and all contents. Evidence requirements and category-specific coverage are explained in our Returns, Replacements & Refund Policy. Old, vintage and resale action figures have no voluntary DIT/LIT coverage. No shipping exclusion or evidence requirement removes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded.
