Cancellation requests

For apparel, headgear and other orders eligible for voluntary cancellation, request cancellation within seven calendar days of placing the order and before dispatch, whichever occurs first. Email nerdverseindia@gmail.com or +91 7003327983 with your order number. We will check dispatch status and confirm the outcome. A request is not itself confirmation of cancellation. No cancellation fee is charged for an accepted cancellation.

Old, vintage, pre-owned and resale action figures remain final sale under our voluntary store policy once the order is confirmed. After dispatch, contact us for assistance; refusing delivery does not automatically entitle you to cancellation or a refund. These terms do not limit remedies available under applicable law.

Damaged, defective, incorrect or materially misdescribed products

Report a damaged, defective or incorrect product, missing contents, or a material difference from its listing within seven calendar days of delivery to nerdverseindia@gmail.com or +91 7003327983. Include your order number, a description and supporting photographs and unboxing video where applicable. We will review the evidence and, where needed, inspect the returned item before confirming the appropriate resolution.

Disclosed vintage wear, disclosed packaging defects and change of mind are distinct from receiving the wrong or materially misdescribed product. The category-specific voluntary restrictions below do not exclude a return, replacement or refund required by applicable law for defective, deficient, spurious or misdescribed goods or an unfulfilled order. A missing unboxing video does not automatically defeat a claim supported by other evidence where statutory rights apply. The seven-day reporting request does not shorten rights or time limits provided by law.

For eligible apparel and headgear claims, replacement is offered first under our voluntary policy; if the required replacement is unavailable, an approved refund is issued. This does not prevent a different remedy where required by applicable law.

Manufacturer warranty

A manufacturer warranty applies only where expressly stated in the listing and subject to its documented terms. Contact us for assistance with an applicable warranty. No manufacturer warranty is implied for vintage or pre-owned items; referral to a manufacturer does not remove our obligations under applicable law.

Return and exchange eligibility

For products eligible under this policy, request a return or exchange within seven calendar days of delivery. This is a request window; the parcel does not need to reach us within those seven days. A timely request remains valid while we review it. After this window, voluntary returns or exchanges are not offered, without limiting rights or remedies available under applicable law.

For a voluntary return or exchange, the item must be unused, unwashed and unaltered, in the condition received, with its original tags, accessories and product packaging. Preserve the outer shipping packaging where possible. A damaged outer shipping box does not by itself invalidate an otherwise eligible damage claim. Inspection and any necessary handling to document an issue do not automatically invalidate a statutory claim.

Apparel and headgear are eligible for size/fit replacements and verified wrong-item or damage claims as described below. Replacement is offered first under the voluntary policy; if the required replacement is unavailable, an approved refund is issued. There is no general change-of-mind refund entitlement under our voluntary policy.

Excluded categories and sale items

Old, vintage, pre-owned and resale action figures are excluded from voluntary returns, exchanges and refunds as described below. Any additional product-specific exclusion must be disclosed clearly in its listing before purchase. Buying an item on sale does not, by itself, remove an otherwise applicable remedy; a sale-item exclusion applies only where disclosed before purchase and permitted by law. Product exclusions do not remove statutory remedies for defective, deficient, spurious or materially misdescribed goods.

Return authorisation, receipt and inspection

Email nerdverseindia@gmail.com with the order number, reason and supporting evidence. Wait for written return authorisation and the return address before shipping. We will email you when the authorised return is received, inspect its condition against the published eligibility criteria, and notify you by email whether the replacement or refund is approved. If a request is declined, we will explain why. An accepted return is then processed under the replacement and refund timelines below. Do not send an item to a manufacturer unless instructed as part of a documented warranty process.

1. Old, vintage and resale action figures: final sale

All old, vintage, pre-owned and resale action figures are strictly non-returnable, non-replaceable and non-refundable under our store policy. All such sales are final once the order is confirmed.

These items are sold in the condition shown and described in their individual listings. Review every photograph, condition note, accessory description and packaging disclosure carefully. Ask for clarification or additional photographs before placing your order.

Age-related characteristics may include paint wear, discolouration, loose joints, hardened or deteriorated components, manufacturing variations, storage wear, card bends, creases, sticker marks, blister cracks and packaging deterioration. A carded or factory-sealed vintage item is not a guarantee of mint condition, working mechanisms or the condition of components concealed inside its packaging. We disclose known issues; these general notes do not replace the individual listing's condition description.

No voluntary returns, replacements or refunds are offered for change of mind, buyer's remorse, personal expectations, disclosed imperfections, age-related wear, or opening a collectible after delivery. The seven-day apparel and headgear replacement window does not apply to these figures.

2. DIT / LIT: not covered for old, vintage and resale figures

DIT (Damage in Transit) and LIT (Loss in Transit) are not covered under our voluntary store policy for old, vintage and resale action figures.

This exclusion covers courier mishandling, transit breakage, damage to the figure or its packaging during shipping, and parcels lost in transit. These events do not qualify for a voluntary store return, replacement or refund for this category. We pack collectibles with care, but protective packaging cannot eliminate transit risk.

Report courier issues promptly through our Contact page, quoting your order number and tracking details, so that available shipment information can be checked. Such assistance does not create additional DIT/LIT coverage under this store policy.

3. Apparel and headgear: seven-day replacement window

Apparel and headgear, including T-shirts and caps, are eligible for replacement requests within seven calendar days of delivery. Returns are for replacement only; a refund cannot be selected when the required replacement is available.

This window covers size or fit replacement requests and reports of wrong items or visible product damage, subject to inspection. Contact us within seven days, stating the order number, reason and requested replacement size or variant. Replacement stock is subject to availability.

Returned items must be unused, unwashed, unaltered and free of stains, odours and signs of wear, with their original tags, labels, accessories and packaging. Trying an item briefly to assess fit must not affect its saleable condition. Items damaged after delivery, washed, altered or returned with missing tags do not qualify under the voluntary replacement policy.

4. Mandatory unboxing video

A clear, continuous and unedited unboxing video is required as supporting evidence for claims involving a wrong item, missing contents or visible damage. Record it before opening the parcel, even if you do not expect an issue.

  • Show the unopened outer parcel, shipping label, order-identifying details and all seals clearly.
  • Record the complete opening process without pauses, cuts, edits or items moving out of view.
  • Show the contents, product tags, packaging and the reported issue clearly.
  • Keep the original video file until the request is resolved and provide it through the support channel supplied for your request.

Photographs may be requested in addition to the video. Do not post your shipping label or personal address publicly. A video is not required for a size/fit-only replacement request. An unboxing video does not make a final-sale figure eligible for voluntary returns, replacements or refunds and does not add DIT/LIT coverage.

5. How to request a replacement

  1. Use our Contact page within seven calendar days of delivery.
  2. Provide your order number, item name, reason for the request, preferred replacement and supporting photographs/video where applicable.
  3. Wait for return instructions before shipping anything. Do not send an item to an address without authorisation.
  4. Pack an authorised return securely and retain the courier receipt and tracking number.
  5. Once the returned item is received and inspected, we will confirm eligibility and replacement availability.

A timely request remains within the seven-day window while it is being reviewed. Approval to send an item back is subject to inspection and is not itself confirmation of a refund. We will explain the reason if a return fails the published eligibility conditions.

6. Return and replacement shipping

For voluntary size or fit replacements, the customer pays the return shipping and replacement delivery charges, communicated before proceeding. For a verified wrong-item or product-damage claim accepted for apparel or headgear, NerdVerse India covers the authorised return and replacement shipping. Contact us before booking a courier; unsolicited courier charges are not automatically reimbursable.

7. Refund eligibility and processing

Under our voluntary apparel or headgear return policy, a refund is issued when the required replacement stock is unavailable. Refunds also apply to accepted cancellations, orders cancelled by the store, verified duplicate payments and claims where a refund is required by applicable law. You are not required to accept an unrelated product or store credit instead.

We initiate an approved refund within seven calendar days of refund approval, following receipt and inspection of an authorised return where a return is required. Refunds go to the original payment method; bank or payment-provider posting times may vary.

The refund covers the amount paid for the eligible returned item after applicable discounts. Original delivery charges for a voluntary size/fit return are not refundable. For a verified wrong-item or product-damage claim, any applicable shipping reimbursement will be confirmed with the resolution. We do not ask for your UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN or password to process a refund.

8. Orders cancelled by the store

If NerdVerse India cannot fulfil an order because of unavailable stock, an unverified payment or a material listing error, we will contact you. Any payment received for an order we cancel will be refunded to the original payment method within seven calendar days of cancellation. This is separate from the post-delivery return policy.

Refund credit timeline and payment issues

After initiation, the bank or payment provider will usually credit the original payment method within 5–10 business days; actual posting time depends on that provider. Email nerdverseindia@gmail.com with your order number if the credit is not received within ten business days of initiation. We will share the available refund reference and help trace it.

If you are charged twice or debited without an order confirmation, email the transaction reference and payment date. We will reconcile the payment and initiate a refund of any verified duplicate or unallocated payment within seven calendar days. Failed-payment reversals are handled by the bank or provider; contact us if the reversal remains outstanding.
