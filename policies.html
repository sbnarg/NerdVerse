STORE POLICIES

Simple rules.
Collector-friendly.

Authenticity

We aim to describe products accurately and disclose known condition, packaging, restoration or reproduction details. Branded products should be represented as genuine only when their provenance has been reasonably verified.

Shipping

Shipping charges, delivery timelines and serviceability will be confirmed at checkout/order confirmation. Fragile collectibles should be packed with suitable protective material.

Returns

For the launch version, returns should be accepted only according to the final product-specific policy shown at order confirmation. Damage-in-transit claims should include clear photos/video of the parcel and item.

Privacy

Customer details should be used only for order processing, support and legally required records. Customer information is used for order processing, delivery coordination, customer support and legally required records. Do not share customer information with unrelated third parties except where needed to provide the service or comply with law.

Terms

Prices, availability and product condition are subject to confirmation until an order is accepted. Limited and vintage pieces may have individual condition notes.
