Privacy Policy

Effective: 7 October 2026

NerdVerse India respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you when you use our website, create an account, or place an order.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, account identifiers, order details and information you choose to provide when contacting us. When you sign in with Google, authentication is handled through Google and Supabase; we receive basic account information such as your name and email address as permitted by you.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, process and fulfil orders, provide order and delivery updates, provide customer support, prevent misuse, maintain store security and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Payments

Payment processing may be provided by third-party payment providers such as Razorpay. NerdVerse India does not intend to store full payment-card credentials on its own website. Payment providers process payment information under their own privacy and security practices.

Service providers

We use service providers for website hosting, authentication, database services, payment processing and related store operations. Information is shared with these providers only as reasonably necessary to provide those services.

Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for account administration, order fulfilment, customer support, fraud prevention and legal or accounting requirements. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact us regarding access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to records we must retain for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or transaction purposes. You may also sign out of your NerdVerse India account at any time.

Children

The store is intended for purchases made by adults. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children for account creation or purchasing.

Changes

We may update this policy as the store, legal requirements or our service providers change. The effective date above will be updated when appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details published on our Contact page.
