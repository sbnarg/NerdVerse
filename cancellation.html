Cancellation requests

For apparel, headgear and other orders eligible for voluntary cancellation, request cancellation within seven calendar days of placing the order and before dispatch, whichever occurs first. Email nerdverseindia@gmail.com or +91 7003327983 with your order number. We will check dispatch status and confirm the outcome. A request is not itself confirmation of cancellation. No cancellation fee is charged for an accepted cancellation.

Old, vintage, pre-owned and resale action figures remain final sale under our voluntary store policy once the order is confirmed. After dispatch, contact us for assistance; refusing delivery does not automatically entitle you to cancellation or a refund. These terms do not limit remedies available under applicable law.

Accepted cancellations and refunds

For an accepted pre-dispatch cancellation, any payment collected for the cancelled order will be refunded to the original payment method. We initiate the refund within seven calendar days of cancellation approval. Bank and payment-provider posting times may vary. No cancellation fee is charged for an accepted cancellation.

Cancellation by NerdVerse India

We may cancel an order that cannot be fulfilled because of unavailable stock, an unverified payment, a material listing error or reasonably suspected fraud. We will notify you and initiate a refund of any payment collected for the cancelled order to the original payment method within seven calendar days.

After delivery

Post-delivery requests follow our Returns, Replacements & Refund Policy. Apparel and headgear have a seven-calendar-day replacement request window. A refund for an eligible return is offered only when the required replacement stock is unavailable.

Applicable rights

Nothing in this policy excludes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded. Final-sale restrictions operate only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Contact

Refund credit timeline and payment issues

Approved refunds are initiated within seven calendar days as described above. After initiation, the bank or payment provider will usually credit the original payment method within 5–10 business days; actual posting time depends on that provider. Email nerdverseindia@gmail.com with your order number if the credit is not received within ten business days of initiation. We will share the available refund reference and help trace it.

If you are charged twice or debited without an order confirmation, email the transaction reference and payment date. We will reconcile the payment and initiate a refund of any verified duplicate or unallocated payment within seven calendar days. Failed-payment reversals are handled by the bank or provider; contact us if the reversal remains outstanding.
