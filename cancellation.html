Apparel, headgear and other eligible orders

Request cancellation within 24 hours of order confirmation and before dispatch, whichever occurs first. Send your order number and cancellation request through the official channels on our Contact page. A request is not confirmed until we acknowledge its acceptance. Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled under our voluntary cancellation policy.

Old, vintage and resale action figures

Old, vintage, pre-owned and resale action figures are final sale once the order is confirmed. No voluntary cancellation, return, replacement or refund is offered for these items. DIT/LIT is not covered under the store policy. Read the full Returns, Replacements & Refund Policy before purchase.

Accepted cancellations and refunds

For an accepted pre-dispatch cancellation, any payment collected for the cancelled order will be refunded to the original payment method. We initiate the refund within seven business days of cancellation approval. Bank and payment-provider posting times may vary. Business days exclude Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays. No cancellation fee is charged for an accepted cancellation.

Cancellation by NerdVerse India

We may cancel an order that cannot be fulfilled because of unavailable stock, an unverified payment, a material listing error or reasonably suspected fraud. We will notify you and initiate a refund of any payment collected for the cancelled order to the original payment method within seven business days.

After delivery

Post-delivery requests follow our Returns, Replacements & Refund Policy. Apparel and headgear have a seven-calendar-day replacement request window. A refund for an eligible return is offered only when the required replacement stock is unavailable.

Applicable rights

Nothing in this policy excludes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded. Final-sale restrictions operate only to the extent permitted by applicable law.
